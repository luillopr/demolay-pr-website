LEADERSHIP BY DOING

Youth-led. Mentor-supported.

DeMolay gives young men meaningful opportunities to plan, communicate, organize and lead, while trained adult advisors provide mentorship and support.

OUR MODEL

Leadership is practiced, not just discussed.

YOUTH

Member leadership

Members elect officers, help set goals and take active roles in chapter programs.

MENTORS

Adult guidance

Adult advisors coach, safeguard and support without replacing the youth leadership experience.

SKILLS

Skills for life

Communication, teamwork, planning, responsibility and public speaking grow through real experience.

SERVICE

Character in action

Leadership is connected to service, citizenship and responsibility to others.
